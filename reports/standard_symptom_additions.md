# Standard symptom additions

Added for standard reference-book coverage based on the NHS Symptoms A to Z review:

- Breast Pain
- Pins and Needles (Paresthesia)
- Itching in Pregnancy and Intrahepatic Cholestasis
- Floaters and Flashes in the Eyes
- Vision Loss
- Fever in Children
- Rashes in Babies and Children
- Reflux in Babies
- Hip Pain in Children
- Limping in Children
- Tailbone (Coccyx) Pain
- Vaginal Bleeding in Pregnancy

These additions emphasize common symptoms, emergency eye presentations, pediatric safety, and pregnancy-specific red flags.
