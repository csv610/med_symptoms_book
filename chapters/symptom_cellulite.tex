\chapter{Cellulite}

\section*{Definition}
Cellulite is a common cosmetic condition characterized by dimpled, uneven skin surface, typically on the thighs, buttocks, and abdomen. It affects 80-90 percent of post-adolescent women.

\section*{What Happens in Your Body}
Cellulite results from structural changes in the subcutaneous fat layer and its fibrous connective tissue. In women, the fibrous septae connecting the dermis to the deep fascia run vertically, creating compartments that allow fat protrusion into the dermis when fat hypertrophies. This creates the characteristic cottage cheese or orange peel appearance.

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Female sex (female-pattern fat distribution)
\item Genetic predisposition
\item Hormonal factors (estrogen, insulin, prolactin)
\item Age-related skin thinning and collagen loss
\item Increased body fat percentage
\item Sedentary lifestyle
\item Diet high in processed foods, salt, and carbohydrates
\item Dehydration (reduced skin elasticity)
\item Pregnancy (hormonal and weight changes)
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to See a Doctor}
\begin{itemize}
\item Dimpled, uneven skin on the thighs, buttocks, hips, or abdomen
\item No associated pain, redness, or systemic symptoms
\item Skin may feel cooler or have reduced sensation
\item No ulceration, discharge, or signs of infection
\end{itemize}

\section*{Related Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
\item Obesity or overweight
\item Skin laxity (loss of skin elasticity)
\item Poor circulation (venous or lymphatic insufficiency)
\item Stretch marks (striae)
\item Lymphedema (different condition)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Self-Care / Home Management}
\begin{itemize}
\item Maintain a healthy weight through balanced diet and regular exercise
\item Strength training to build muscle and improve skin tone
\item Stay well-hydrated to maintain skin elasticity
\item Dry brushing to stimulate circulation
\item Topical creams containing caffeine or retinol (temporary improvement)
\item Massage therapy to improve local circulation
\item Accept that cellulite is a normal variation
\end{itemize}

\section*{How Your Doctor Will Evaluate You}
\begin{itemize}
\item Clinical diagnosis based on visual inspection
\item No laboratory or imaging tests are necessary
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment Options}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lifestyle modification: weight loss, exercise, healthy diet
\item Topical treatments (retinol, caffeine) minimal temporary benefit
\item Laser and radiofrequency therapies for mild improvement
\item Subcision (Cellfina) to release fibrous septae
\item Acoustic wave therapy
\item Endermologie (mechanical massage) for temporary reduction
\end{itemize}
