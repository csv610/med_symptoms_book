\chapter{Frequent Urination (Urinary Frequency)}

\section{Definition}
Frequent urination is the need to urinate more often than normal. The typical adult urinates 4-8 times per day. Causes range from benign lifestyle factors to serious medical conditions.

\section{Pathophysiology}
Urinary frequency occurs when the bladder cannot hold the normal volume of urine (decreased functional capacity), the body produces excess urine (polyuria), or the bladder is hypersensitive. Mechanisms include: detrusor overactivity, bladder outlet obstruction, increased fluid intake, diabetes (polydipsia), urinary tract infection (irritation), or bladder compression.

\section{Etiology}
\begin{itemize}
\item Urinary tract infection (most common cause of acute frequency)
\item Overactive bladder (detrusor overactivity)
\item Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) in men
\item Diabetes mellitus (polyuria from hyperglycemia)
\item Diabetes insipidus (excessive dilute urine)
\item Diuretic use (medications, caffeine, alcohol)
\item Excessive fluid intake (polydipsia)
\item Pregnancy (compression from uterus)
\item Pelvic organ prolapse
\item Interstitial cystitis (painful bladder syndrome)
\item Bladder stones or tumors
\item Radiation cystitis
\item Anxiety or habit
\end{itemize}

\section{Indications for Evaluation}
\begin{itemize}
\item Urinating more than 8 times in 24 hours
\item Waking up to urinate (nocturia)
\item Sudden onset of frequency (think UTI or diabetes)
\item Pain or burning with urination
\item Excessive thirst and increased fluid intake
\item Incomplete bladder emptying
\item Change in urine color or odor
\item Fever or back pain
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
\item Urinary urgency
\item Nocturia (nighttime urination)
\item Dysuria (painful urination)
\item Polyuria (excessive urine output)
\item Urinary incontinence
\end{itemize}

\section{Self-Care/Home Management}
\begin{itemize}
\item Reduce caffeine and alcohol intake
\item Limit fluids before bedtime
\item Bladder training: gradually increase intervals between urination
\item Pelvic floor exercises
\item Keep a bladder diary to track patterns
\item Avoid bladder irritants (spicy foods, citrus, artificial sweeteners)
\end{itemize}

\section{Diagnostic Approach}
\begin{itemize}
\item Urinalysis and urine culture
\item Bladder diary
\item Blood glucose and HbA1c
\item Post-void residual measurement
\item Urodynamic studies if indicated
\end{itemize}

\section{Treatment/Management Overview}
\begin{itemize}
\item Treat underlying cause (antibiotics for UTI, diabetes management, BPH treatment)
\item Behavioral therapy (bladder training, pelvic floor therapy)
\item Anticholinergic or beta-3 agonist medications for OAB
\item Avoid excessive fluid intake
\end{itemize}
