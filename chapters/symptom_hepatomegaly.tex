\chapter{Hepatomegaly}

\section*{Definition}
Hepatomegaly is enlargement of the liver beyond normal size, defined as a liver span greater than 12--15 cm at the midclavicular line (by percussion) or greater than 15.5 cm (by ultrasound). A palpable liver below the costal margin in adults is abnormal, though the right lobe may be palpable 1--2 cm below in deep inspiration.

\section*{What Happens in Your Body}
Hepatomegaly results from hepatocellular hypertrophy or hyperplasia, infiltration (fat, glycogen, amyloid), congestion (venous outflow obstruction), or neoplastic growth. The liver capsule stretches, causing a dull ache or fullness in the right upper quadrant.

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fatty liver disease:} Nonalcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD, most common in Western countries), alcoholic liver disease, obesity, diabetes, hyperlipidemia
  \item \textbf{Viral hepatitis:} Acute (HAV, HBV, HBV+HDV, HEV) and chronic (HBV, HCV) infection
  \item \textbf{Congestive:} Heart failure (passive congestion), Budd-Chiari syndrome (hepatic vein thrombosis), constrictive pericarditis, tricuspid regurgitation
  \item \textbf{Infiltrative:} Hemochromatosis, Gaucher disease, glycogen storage diseases, amyloidosis, Wilson disease
  \item \textbf{Neoplastic:} Hepatocellular carcinoma, metastatic disease (most common malignant cause), hepatic adenoma, focal nodular hyperplasia
  \item \textbf{Other:} Sarcoidosis, abscess (pyogenic, amebic), extrahepatic biliary obstruction, polycystic liver disease, granulomatous hepatitis
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to See a Doctor}
See your doctor if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Right upper quadrant pain, tenderness, or palpable mass
  \item Jaundice, dark urine, or pale stools suggesting biliary obstruction
  \item Unexplained weight loss, fever, night sweats, or anorexia
  \item Known cirrhosis or chronic liver disease with sudden increase in liver size
  \item Signs of liver failure: ascites, spider angiomas, palmar erythema, asterixis, encephalopathy
\end{itemize}

\section*{Related Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Right upper quadrant pain or fullness, jaundice, pruritus, nausea, fatigue, early satiety, leg edema, ascites
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Important:} Hepatomegaly may be an incidental finding on imaging; the presence of symptoms depends on the underlying cause and rate of enlargement.

\section*{Self-Care / Home Management}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Avoid alcohol entirely if liver disease is suspected or confirmed
  \item Maintain a healthy weight through diet and exercise to reduce hepatic steatosis
  \item Review medications for potential hepatotoxins (acetaminophen limit 2 g/day, statins, methotrexate, amiodarone)
  \item Vaccinate against HAV and HBV in people with chronic liver disease
  \item Follow dietary recommendations for underlying condition (low-salt for congestive hepatomegaly, diabetic diet for NAFLD)
\end{itemize}

\section*{How Your Doctor Will Evaluate You}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Liver span measurement by percussion and palpation; assess surface contour, nodularity, tenderness
  \item Liver function tests (ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, bilirubin) and synthetic function (albumin, INR)
  \item Abdominal ultrasound with Doppler: size, echotexture, masses, hepatic vein patency, fatty infiltration
  \item Viral hepatitis serologies (HAV IgM, HBsAg, HBsAb, HBcAb, HCV Ab, HCV RNA)
  \item Liver biopsy for unexplained hepatomegaly, infiltrative disease, or grading of fibrosis
  \item Iron studies, ceruloplasmin, alpha-1 antitrypsin, ANA/anti-mitochondrial antibodies for specific etiologies
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment Options}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Treat underlying cause: weight loss and insulin sensitizers for NAFLD; antivirals for HBV/HCV; chelation for hemochromatosis
  \item Diuretics and salt restriction for congestive hepatomegaly from heart failure
  \item Anticoagulation or TIPS for Budd-Chiari syndrome; liver transplantation for fulminant or end-stage disease
  \item Abscess drainage (percutaneous or surgical) plus antibiotics for pyogenic liver abscess
  \item Resection, ablation, or transplantation for hepatocellular carcinoma based on Barcelona Clinic Liver Cancer (BCLC) staging
\end{itemize}
