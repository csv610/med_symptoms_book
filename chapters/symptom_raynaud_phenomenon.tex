\chapter{Raynaud Phenomenon}

\section*{Definition}
Episolic vasospastic ischemia of digital arteries in response to cold or stress, producing well-demarcated triphasic color changes (white from vasospasm, blue from cyanosis, red from reperfusion hyperemia); typically bilateral in primary Raynaud and asymmetric in secondary disease.

\section*{What Happens in Your Body}
Exaggerated digital narrowing of blood vessels results from hyperactivity of alpha-2 adrenergic receptors and impaired endothelial widening of blood vessels (reduced nitric oxide bioavailability). Primary Raynaud has normal vessel architecture, while secondary Raynaud involves structural microvascular damage (intimal hyperplasia, capillary dropout, thrombosis) from underlying connective tissue disease or cryoglobulinemia.

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Primary Raynaud disease: No identifiable cause, age of onset before 30 years, normal nailfold capillaries, negative serology
  \item Connective tissue diseases: Scleroderma (limited/diffuse), systemic lupus erythematosus, mixed connective tissue disease, Sj\"ogren syndrome, dermatomyositis
  \item Vascular disorders: Cryoglobulinemia, cryofibrinogenemia, Buerger disease, atherosclerosis, thoracic outlet syndrome, repeated vibration injury (hand-arm vibration syndrome)
  \item Medications: Beta-blockers, ergotamines, sumatriptan, cisplatin, bleomycin, interferon-alpha, oral contraceptives
  \item Endocrine: Hypothyroidism, pheochromocytoma (paroxysmal)
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to See a Doctor}
See your doctor if:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Unilateral symptoms, onset after age 30, or severe attacks with ischemic pain
  \item Digital ulceration, pitting scars, gangrene, or sclerodactyly (tight skin on fingers)
  \item Associated with arthritis, rash, myositis, dysphagia, or unexplained fever
  \item Positive antinuclear antibody (ANA) or other autoantibodies on prior testing
\end{itemize}

\section*{Related Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Digital numbness or tingling during attacks
  \item Throbbing pain during rewarming (reperfusion phase)
  \item Telangiectasias, calcinosis cutis (in limited scleroderma/CREST syndrome)
  \item Sclerodactyly, Raynaud plus esophageal dysmotility
  \item Finger swelling or joint stiffness
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Important:} Nailfold capillaroscopy is a simple bedside test that differentiates primary (normal loops) from secondary (dilated, tortuous, or dropout capillaries) Raynaud.

\section*{Self-Care / Home Management}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Keep entire body warm: layered clothing, gloves, warm socks, hand warmers, and avoid rapid temperature changes.
  \item Avoid vasoconstrictive substances: nicotine (smoking cessation critical), caffeine, decongestants, and ergotamines.
  \item Perform stress-reduction techniques (biofeedback, deep breathing) as emotional triggers can provoke attacks.
  \item Avoid injury to fingers and protect hands during cold exposure.
  \item For acute attacks, use warm (not hot) water or body heat from armpits to rewarm gently.
\end{itemize}

\section*{How Your Doctor Will Evaluate You}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Nailfold capillaroscopy to assess capillary morphology (dilation, dropout, hemorrhage).
  \item Antinuclear antibody (ANA), anti-Scl-70 (topoisomerase I), anti-centromere, anti-U1 RNP serology.
  \item Complete blood count, ESR, CRP, complement levels, cryoglobulins, and cold agglutinins.
  \item Digital blood pressure, laser Doppler, or thermography for objective vasospasm documentation.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment Options}
\begin{itemize}
  \item First-line: Dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (nifedipine ER 30-90 mg daily or amlodipine 5-10 mg daily).
  \item Second-line: Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil), topical nitrates, losartan, fluoxetine.
  \item Severe/ulcerative: IV prostacyclin analogs (iloprost, epoprostenol), bosentan (endothelin receptor antagonist) for digital ulcers in systemic sclerosis.
  \item Surgical: Digital sympathectomy for refractory ischemic digits; avoid amputation until maximal medical therapy.
\end{itemize}
