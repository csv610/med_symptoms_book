\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This book is a comprehensive reference guide to the signs and symptoms that form the foundation of clinical medicine. It is designed for anyone who wants to understand what symptoms mean, what causes them, what happens inside the body, and how they are evaluated and managed.

The book covers 474 symptoms, organized alphabetically and presented in a consistent structure. Each chapter begins with a clear definition of the symptom, followed by an explanation of the underlying pathophysiology, common and serious causes, guidance on when to seek medical care, practical self-care measures, a description of the diagnostic evaluation a doctor would perform, an overview of treatment options, and a list of related symptoms. A reference section at the end of each chapter directs the reader to authoritative sources for further reading.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{How to Use This Book}

This book is intended as a reference, not a diagnostic tool. If you are experiencing a symptom, look it up by name and read the relevant chapter to understand possible causes and what steps to take. The ``When to See a Doctor'' section in each chapter provides clear guidance on when professional evaluation is needed. The ``Self-Care / Home Management'' section offers practical advice for symptoms that can be managed at home.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Important Disclaimer}

The information in this book is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in this book.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately.
