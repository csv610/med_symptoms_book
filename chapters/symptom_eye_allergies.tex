\chapter{Eye Allergies (Allergic Conjunctivitis)}

\section*{Definition}
Eye allergies (allergic conjunctivitis) are inflammation of the conjunctiva triggered by exposure to allergens such as pollen, dust mites, pet dander, or mold. It is the most common cause of eye allergy symptoms and is estimated to affect 15-20 percent of the population.

\section*{What Happens in Your Body}
When an allergen contacts the conjunctiva, it cross-links IgE antibodies on the surface of mast cells, triggering degranulation and release of histamine and other inflammatory mediators. Histamine causes widening of blood vessels (redness), increased vascular permeability (swelling and tearing), and nerve stimulation (itching).

\section*{Causes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Seasonal pollens (ragweed, grass, tree pollen)
\item House dust mites
\item Pet dander (cats, dogs)
\item Mold spores
\item Cockroach allergens
\item Contact lens solutions or preservatives
\item Air pollution or smoke
\item Cosmetics or eye makeup
\end{itemize}

\section*{When to See a Doctor}
\begin{itemize}
\item Eye itching (most characteristic symptom)
\item Redness of the eyes
\item Watery or stringy discharge
\item Swollen eyelids
\item Burning sensation
\item Sensitivity to light (photophobia)
\item Nasal congestion and sneezing (often concurrent)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Related Symptoms}
\begin{itemize}
\item Itchy eyes
\item Red eyes
\item Watery eyes
\item Swollen eyelids
\item Allergic rhinitis (hay fever)
\item Conjunctivitis (infectious)
\item Contact dermatitis of the eyelids
\end{itemize}

\section*{Self-Care / Home Management}
\begin{itemize}
\item Avoid known allergens
\item Wear wrap-around sunglasses outdoors
\item Use artificial tears (preservative-free) to flush allergens
\item Apply cold compresses to itchy eyes
\item Remove contact lenses and switch to glasses
\item Wash hands after touching pets
\item Shower and change clothes after outdoor exposure
\end{itemize}

\section*{How Your Doctor Will Evaluate You}
\begin{itemize}
\item Clinical history and eye examination
\item Slit-lamp examination of the conjunctiva
\item Allergy skin testing or specific IgE blood tests
\item Conjunctival scraping for eosinophils in chronic cases
\end{itemize}

\section*{Treatment Options}
\begin{itemize}
\item Antihistamine eye drops (olopatadine, ketotifen)
\item Mast cell stabilizer eye drops (cromolyn sodium)
\item Combination drops (antihistamine + mast cell stabilizer)
\item Topical NSAID eye drops
\item Lubricating eye drops (artificial tears)
\item Oral antihistamines
\item Topical corticosteroid eye drops for severe cases
\end{itemize}
